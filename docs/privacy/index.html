This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Bee Mosaic (the “Game”, package identifier com.ragendom.beemosaic) on Android, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and what rights you have over it.

Because the controller is established in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) applies to this Game worldwide.

2. The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never create one, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device, not on our servers. We do not operate any servers of our own.
  • Third-party tools inside the Game do collect data — Google AdMob for advertising, Google Play Billing for purchases, and the Unity engine for basic device and stability information. Each is named in section 4.
  • Personalised advertising only happens if you consent to it, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • We never see your payment details. Google Play handles all payments.
  • The Game is not intended for children under 13.

3. Information you give us directly

None, in normal use. The Game has no registration, no login, no profile and no user-generated content. Your level progress, boosters and settings are saved in local storage on your own device and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

If you contact us by email for support or to exercise a privacy right, we will hold your email address and the contents of your message for as long as needed to deal with it (see section 10).

4. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) supplied by other companies. These collect data directly from your device. We name each one below together with a link to its own privacy policy.

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

Provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. The Game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video advertisements through AdMob and its mediated ad partners. AdMob may collect:

  • your device advertising identifier — the Android Advertising ID (AAID);
  • IP address, and an approximate location (typically country or city level) derived from it;
  • device information: model, operating system version, language, screen characteristics, network type;
  • advertising interactions: which ads were shown, viewed, clicked, completed or closed;
  • signals used for fraud prevention, invalid-traffic detection and frequency capping.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ad partners used through AdMob mediation are listed in the consent dialog described in section 6.

4.2 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. The Game offers optional purchases: an advertisement-removal entitlement, packs of the in-game currency (“Coins”) and bundles of Coins and booster charges. Each purchase is made, validated and restored through Google Play’s billing system, which may process:

  • your Google account and payment details — handled entirely by Google; we never receive, see or store them;
  • purchase tokens, product identifiers, purchase state and purchase time, which the Game reads to deliver what you bought and to restore non-consumable purchases on reinstall. Coins and booster charges themselves are stored only on your device.

Google also provides us with aggregate, non-identifying statistics about installs and sales. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

4.3 Unity engine and Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies)

The Game is built with the Unity engine, provided by Unity Technologies ApS (Denmark) and Unity Technologies, Inc. (United States). The engine and its purchasing module may collect:

  • a randomly generated installation identifier, which is not your name and is reset if you reinstall the Game or clear its data;
  • device model, operating system version, CPU and graphics hardware, screen size, language and country/region derived from IP address, Game version;
  • basic stability and diagnostic information, such as whether the Game crashed on launch, used to keep the Game working.

See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4.4 Google Play (distribution)

The Game is distributed through Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Google Play may show us aggregate, anonymised statistics about installs, uninstalls, crashes and ratings; these never identify you to us. The Game may occasionally ask whether you would like to rate it; this prompt is the Google Play In-App Review feature, provided by Google, and any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play under its own terms. The Game does not learn whether or what you rated.

6. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free and is funded by advertising. You are always able to control whether advertising is personalised.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Before any advertising identifier is used for personalisation, a consent dialog is shown using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), which lists the advertising partners involved and lets you accept or reject each purpose. If you decline, you will still be able to play the entire Game; the advertisements you see will be non-personalised. You can reopen this dialog at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the Game and change your answer.

On Android

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation entirely, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version).

Everywhere

You can opt out of personalised Google advertising at adssettings.google.com. Purchasing the advertisement-removal item stops banner and interstitial advertisements; optional rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for advertising;
  • data portability for data processed by consent or contract;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

How to delete your data

Immediately, by yourself:

  • Uninstall the Game — this removes all locally stored progress from your device.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings (section 6) — this severs the link between your device and past advertising data.
  • Withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy Options inside the Game.

By request: email ragendom@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request – Bee Mosaic”. Because the Game has no accounts, please include your device model and, if you have made a purchase, the Google Play order number, so that the correct records can be located. We will respond within one month as required by Article 12(3) GDPR.

Exercising any of these rights is free of charge. We may ask for information to confirm the request relates to you.

Supervisory authority

If you are in the European Union you may complain to your local data protection authority. The authority for Hungary, where the controller is established, is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH) — naih.hu, where current postal and electronic contact details are published.

12. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the use of advertising identifiers for personalised advertising may be treated as a “sale” or “sharing” under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and as “targeted advertising” under comparable laws in other US states.

The categories of personal information involved are identifiers (advertising ID, installation ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay and advertising interactions), approximate geolocation (country or city derived from IP address), and commercial information (purchases made).

You may exercise your right to opt out at any time using the in-game Settings → Privacy Options control, your device advertising settings, or by emailing ragendom@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.
